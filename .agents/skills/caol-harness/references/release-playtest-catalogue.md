## Movement regression coverage

Use the actual production planner and native executor, not just an isolated distance helper.
Attach these conditions to C2 and the affected C3/C4 caller; use small automated variations
for combinations that do not need another full native story.

- Straight and diagonal legs, with normal floor and ordinary difficult terrain, at/below/above
  the executor's actual route horizon. The RAID finding planned48 tiles while native circular
  distance was about65 against max50; a straight-route shortcut can hide the mismatch.
  Prove the caller control on the triggering terrain, then the corrected actual movement.
- Real detours, doors and supported stairs/bridges; distinguish actor route z from signal z.
  A blocked route must not fabricate progress. Reopening or changed occupancy must permit a
  justified retry; repeated unchanged waiting is not recovery evidence.
- Complete pair ingress and forward movement, one member loaded/one unloaded, a larger party,
  survivor/zero-survivor disposition and return. Check actual positions and once-only ownership,
  not merely a travelling label. Use the retained-pair regression in bandit-journeys.md.
- Combat/retreat supersedes travel appropriately; surviving actors can resume or resolve their
  journey. A genuine threat-induced pause is not automatically a stuck scout.
- Home approach, cargo/report settlement and release, followed by another useful outing.
  Preserve identity, casualty and inventory across real save/exit/reload at a transfer boundary.
- Independent activation at the selected boundary: a distant home must not freeze an active
  party; truly inactive actors/clocks stay suspended without catch-up or duplicated movement.
- Safe route versus burning source/unsafe neighboring route, spread, all-blocked and reopening.
  Score human and Mi-go sweep callers separately; a searched site does not pass hazard behavior.
